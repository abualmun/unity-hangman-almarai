**🎮 Interactive Educational Game Design**
Developed a digital "fill in the blanks" game inspired by Hangman to promote Almarai Fresh Milk. The game challenges users to guess a unified phrase, reinforcing healthy habits through playful engagement.

---

**🧠 Game Mechanics with Visual Feedback**
Each correct letter pours milk into a virtual glass, providing instant visual and sound feedback. The glass fills up with each correct guess, while incorrect answers trigger humorous cow sounds and sad animations, enhancing user interaction.

---

**🌍 Bilingual Experience & Brand Integration**
The app supports English and Arabic phrases (“Milk every day is the smart way” / "شرب الحليب سر اللبيب") to ensure wide accessibility. Visuals and audio are tightly integrated with Almarai’s branding to maximize engagement and recall.
